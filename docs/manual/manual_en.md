# Part A — Understanding the business

**The market exists and has been measured; the edge lies in picking the right type of purchase. What's missing is the cost.**

## A1. How the business works
**You don't sell to people: you respond to public purchases that have already been decided.**

1. The city government (its Social Assistance department or a municipal Fund) publishes the purchase on the PNCP.
2. The ACOLHE system, a program already built by the person who prepared this manual, downloads the purchases and throws out what doesn't fit; you get a spreadsheet and decide.
3. You read the edital (the purchase document) and the termo de referência (the description of the items) and submit a proposal through the platform (the bidding website).
4. Won? Wait for the empenho, which under an ata de registro de preços (price-registration record) may never come. **You buy only once you have the empenho**, and only bid if the delivery deadline covers your time to buy, assemble and ship.
5. You buy in SP, assemble, issue the electronic invoice (NF-e) and ship.
6. The city gives the atesto (written confirmation that it received the right goods) and pays by bank payment order.

## D3. The cutoff, in plain words
**A batch passes if, once everything is paid, enough is left for your hours and fixed costs, and if your money can fund it.**

1. **Surplus per kit** = price × (1 − tax − platform fee) − materials − shipping − assembly.
2. **Passes** if surplus per kit × kits ≥ hourly value × 8 hours (assumption) + fixed cost per order.
3. **Fits** if (materials + shipping + assembly) × kits + DAS set aside ≤ your money.

Tax: Simples at 4% up to R$ 180 thousand in revenue over 12 months; in the 1st year the band is worked out from the average of the months in business and can come out a little higher (confirm with the contador). Platform: zero on Compras.gov.br, up to 1,5% on BLL. Fixed cost per order: R$ 196 to R$ 473 ÷ orders in the month.

### Example with the measured price
**At R$ 100 per hour, each kit must leave at least R$ 12,73, but the real limit is money: a batch of 100 kits at the maximum cost (R$ 214) needs about R$ 22.400, more than your R$ 12.894.**

- Hour at R$ 100, fixed cost of R$ 473: the batch must leave R$ 100 × 8 + R$ 473 = R$ 1.273, or R$ 12,73 per kit.
- Typical aviso: R$ 236,72 per kit; minus 4% Simples, R$ 227,25 (Compras.gov.br; on BLL, take off up to another 1,5%).
- Minus R$ 12,73: materials + shipping + assembly up to R$ 214,52 per kit.
- Only smaller batches or much cheaper kits fit; the measurement will tell.

| Measurement result | Decision |
|---|---|
| The typical aviso kit passes **and** the batch fits your money | **GO.** It works on paper; only real bids will show the win rate and the real payment time. |
| The typical kit doesn't pass, or the batch doesn't fit | **STOP the kit. Don't open the company.** The person who prepared this manual will measure medals (another product city governments buy) the same way before any spending. |

## D4. How much money you need
**Working capital is the money that pays for goods while the city hasn't paid you yet; the number of days your money sits tied up decides how far it stretches.**

:::CAPITAL FORMULA
- Days of tied-up money (the float): from paying the supplier to getting paid by the city.
- Batches running at the same time = wins per year × days of tied-up money ÷ 365.
- Money needed = batches running at the same time × money per batch.
:::

| Your money | Left after opening | How much you can spend per kit (materials + shipping), batch of 100 |
|---|---|---|
| R$ 12.894 (top) | R$ 11.881 to 12.675 | up to R$ 119 to 127 |
| R$ 2.579 (bottom) | R$ 1.566 to 2.360 | up to R$ 16 to 24: no kit fits |

**One win a week doesn't fit either of these amounts:** with 45 days of tied-up money (assumption) that would mean 6,4 batches running at once (with 75 days, 10,7), and you would need to win 37,4% of avisos (52 of 139), which nobody knows is possible. Start with one open order at a time.

**Cutting the tied-up days in half is like doubling your money, and it costs nothing:** supplier payment terms (none of the suppliers researched offers them today; MaxQualy publishes 7 days on hygiene products, Brascol offers installments for certain CNAEs; none tested), valid certidões, a perfect NF-e, a chased atesto.

**The tax may be due before the money arrives.** The DAS is due on the 20th of the month after the NF-e (confirm with the contador), up to 2 to 4 weeks before payment. On a R$ 24.870 contract, it is R$ 994,80 at 4% (a little more is possible in the 1st year): keep it set aside.

# Part E — Caution

**Know what can kill the business, what isn't known yet and how much to trust each number.**

## E1. What can kill the business
**The biggest risk is the standard kit's cost. The second is missing short windows.**

| Risk | Why it matters | Defense |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong order | Half of real purchases lose money or make almost nothing | Cutoff, order by order |
| Late sweep | Half of avisos close in about 5 days, 29% within 3; the loss is silent | Daily system with failure alert |
| Deadline and distance | A 5-day deadline only works in SP; 18 states unmeasured | Measure PR and GO first |
| Product rejected | A 24–48 h replacement can't get out of SP by PAC | Check color, size and volume |
| A won purchase you can't fulfill | Fine and up to 3 years barred from selling to that agency | Confirm the supplier beforehand |
| Money | R$ 2.579 to 12.894; tax due before payment | One order at a time |
| Single supplier | Emilio: 65,7% of the tested kit's materials | Second source |
| Kit replaced by cash | CNAS 213/2025 prefers cash, still allows kits; deadline 28/10/2026 | Check whether purchases drop |
| Lower demand | No federal funding; states buying jointly; fewer births | Compare with last year |

## E2. What isn't known yet
**Only real bids measure the win rate and the real payment time. Everything else can be measured beforehand.**

| Not known yet | How to find out |
|---|---|
| Cost at wholesale prices | Ask Emilio for the price list |
| Win rate and real payment time | Only by bidding and logging |
| Assembly time; space at home | Time 10 kits; measure the space |
| Shipping to inland cities; acceptance of a private atestado | Prices to real addresses; try it |

## E3. How far the numbers hold
**The numbers are for deciding the next step, not for promising profit.**

| The number | What it is NOT |
|---|---|
| 86,8% in avisos | A large or random sample: it's 19, from one data pull |
| Payment in 30 days | Observed payment: it's the written maximum |
| Materials and shipping prices | Quotes: they're catalog and calculator figures |
| Window of about 5 days | Checked edital by edital: it was read from PNCP dates |

## E4. What we've already corrected
**Almost every old error came from using one case's number in a different case.**

| It used to say | The correct version |
|---|---|
| The gross margin in older versions | Retired: it compared one kit's cost with other kits' prices |
| All dispensas can be bid on | Only the 139 avisos; the 133 atos have no window |
| Dispensas almost never ask for an atestado | Never counted for dispensas; 7 of 8 editais tested (pregões) ask for one. Plan on having one. |

# Words you will hear

**Each term on one line, Portuguese term first, in Portuguese alphabetical order.**

- **Ata de registro de preços (SRP; price-registration record):** price fixed for 12 months (up to 24); the city orders if and when it wants, and you deliver even if your cost has risen. May require a balance sheet (in the 1st year, the opening one).
- **Atestado (reference letter):** a customer's statement that you delivered well.
- **Atesto (delivery sign-off):** written confirmation that the delivery arrived correct.
- **Ato (de dispensa):** a dispensa with the supplier already chosen, no window.
- **Aviso (de dispensa):** a dispensa open to proposals.
- **Balanço (balance sheet):** accounting summary of what the company owns and owes; the contador prepares it.
- **Boleto a prazo (supplier credit):** paying the supplier some days later.
- **Capital de giro (working capital):** money that pays for the goods until you get paid.
- **Carga fracionada (LTL freight):** a truck shared with other loads.
- **Certidão (clearance certificate):** proof that the company has no outstanding debts; it expires.
- **CNAE:** the company's activity code.
- **CNPJ:** the company's registration number with the Receita Federal (federal tax authority).
- **Contador (accountant):** service provider who handles taxes and the balance sheet.
- **CRF:** the FGTS (employee severance fund) certidão, required even without employees.
- **DAS:** the monthly Simples payment slip.
- **Dias de dinheiro parado (float):** the time from paying the supplier until you get paid.
- **Dias úteis (business days):** Monday to Friday, excluding holidays.
- **Dispensa:** a small public purchase, made without a full tender.
- **e-CNPJ:** the company's digital certificate.
- **Edital:** the purchase document, with its rules and deadlines.
- **Empenho:** money formally reserved to pay you; your signal to buy.
- **Estimativa (estimate):** the price the buyer calculated.
- **Habilitação (qualification):** documents proving the company is allowed to sell.
- **Homologação (award confirmation):** the city officially confirms who won.
- **Impugnação (challenge):** a free request to correct the edital.
- **Margem (margin):** what's left after all costs.
- **ME (microempresa):** micro-business (up to R$ 360 thousand per year), with advantages in public purchasing.
- **Mediana (median):** the middle value (half above, half below).
- **MEI:** individual micro-entrepreneur; not usable here.
- **Montadora (co-packer):** a company (with a CNPJ) that assembles kits to order.
- **Nota de corte (cutoff):** the minimum an order must leave to pay for your hours.
- **Nota fiscal (NF-e):** the electronic sales invoice, carrying the empenho number.
- **Ordem bancária (bank payment order):** the payment being sent to you.
- **Ordem de fornecimento (OF; supply order):** the city's formal order.
- **PAC / SEDEX:** Correios economy / express delivery.
- **Plataforma (platform):** the bidding website (Compras.gov.br, BLL, BNC, PCP).
- **PNCP:** the national portal where purchases are published.
- **Pontos percentuais (percentage points):** the difference between two percentages (86,8% − 64,7% = 22).
- **Pregão:** a price auction, sometimes live.
- **Recurso (appeal):** a formal challenge to a decision.
- **SICAF:** the federal supplier registry; some certificates you must resend yourself.
- **Simples Nacional:** the simplified tax regime, paid in a single slip.
- **SLU:** a single-owner limited company.
- **Termo de referência (terms of reference):** description of the items and the delivery.
- **Varredura (sweep):** the system's daily read of the PNCP.
