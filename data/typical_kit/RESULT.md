# What a real kit dispensa leaves — 31 deals, measured

**Measured 2026-09-25.** 31 homologated per-item kit dispensas (of 42 exported; excluded: 2 lot-total artefacts, Teresina above the dispensa ceiling, and 8 where under 90% of the revenue could be costed).

- **Revenue** = what the town actually paid, on the costed lines only.
- **Goods** = qty × article cost at São Paulo catalogue prices (single-unit list prices, no wholesale discount); all 121 article prices re-checked by a second agent (76 confirmed, 37 corrected, 8 unreachable).
- **Freight** = the cheapest public-calculator quote (Melhor Envio / Correios) from CEP 03005-000 to the town for the whole lot, packed several kits per carton; 37 of 39 quotes reproduced by a second agent, 2 corrected. Carton counts are estimates from item volumes.
- **Net** = revenue × 0,96 (Simples 4%) − goods − freight − R$ 473 fixed month. Assembly time, the 1,2% IR-withholding risk and platform fees are not deducted.
- **Best items** = only the lines that make money on goods, freight pro-rated by revenue. Assumes per-item dispensas let her quote a subset of items — UNVERIFIED, check each edital.

## Result

- Middle deal, whole kit: **≈ R$ 100**. 16 of 31 positive; 15 lose money.
- **14 of 31 clear the pass mark** (net ≥ R$ 800 = 8 h at R$ 100/h): median **R$ 4.192**; best four R$ 14.626–22.266.
- Those 14 need a median **R$ 18.950** of cash (goods + freight + DAS); 5 of them fit a R$ 12.894 budget.
- Freight is a median 14,2% of revenue. Losses come from articles the winner clearly sources far below SP retail (bags, going-home sets, apparel 3-packs); profit from articles towns pay well above SP cost (tubs, bottle kits, hooded towels).
- Best-items-only: 21 of 31 positive, median R$ 901.

**Verdict:** the kit business works only as a selection business — bid the minority of dispensas that clear the pass mark, and fund the big ones. The whole-market average is break-even. Upside not counted: wholesale prices (Emilio's revenda table). Downside not counted: assembly time, IR withholding, win rate (unknown; these are the winners' prices).
