## HOW TO ACTUALLY FIND LISTINGS

The big portals (VivaReal, ZAP, Imovelweb, QuintoAndar, OLX) are behind Cloudflare and block automated access. These work and, crucially, publish **rent and condomínio together**:

- `chavesnamao.com.br/apartamentos-para-alugar/sp-sao-paulo/<bairro>/2-quartos/` — gives street addresses; detail pages give IPTU and amenities. It also syndicates QuintoAndar's stock.
- `chavesnamao.com.br/apartamentos-para-alugar/sp-sao-paulo/bairros/<rua-slug>/` — **search by street**. The single best trick.
- `chavesnamao.com.br/apartamentos-para-alugar-direto-com-o-proprietario/sp-sao-paulo/<bairro>/` — owner-direct, no agency fee.
- Four sibling sites, one per zone — using the wrong one silently returns the wrong side of the city: **zoimovel** = Oeste, **zsimovel** = Sul, **znimovel** = Norte, **zlimovel** = Leste. znimovel has a genuine portaria-24h filter per bairro.
- `crimebrasil.com.br/bairro/sp/sao-paulo/<bairro>` for crime per 100k.
- `quintoandar.com.br/regioes-atendidas/...` for resident surveys on safety and lighting (note the sample sizes; anything under n=25 is unreliable).

Note: chavesnamao ignores `?pagina=2` — you only ever see the first ~15 of hundreds. Query many bairro and street slugs instead of paginating. **There is substantial unexplored inventory, and cheaper doorman units may well be sitting in it.**
